Privacy

Last updated 28 November 2026.

The short version

Your homework, timetable, assessments, focus sessions and PIN stay on your own computers. They sync directly between your Mac and PC over your home network, encrypted, and never pass through ClassGuard's servers.

You can use ClassGuard without an account. An account is only needed to connect your devices automatically and, later, for a subscription.

What the ClassGuard account stores

WhatWhy
Your email address (from Google, or the email you sign in with)To know which devices are yours and to let you sign in.
Each signed-in device: its name (e.g. "Sam's PC"), Mac or Windows, ClassGuard version, a public device key and when it was last seenSo your devices can find each other and you can remove old ones.
Short-lived connection requests between your devicesEncrypted on your Mac so that only your PC can read them; ClassGuard can't. Deleted once used, and after 15 minutes at most.
Your plan: free, trial (and when it ends) or ClassGuard Pro, whether it's monthly or yearly, and when it renews or endsTo know what your account includes. Your computers receive a signed copy so Pro keeps working offline.
If you subscribe: a Stripe customer ID and the subscription's statusTo link your payment to your account. Nothing else about the payment is stored here.

The account service is hosted by Supabase in Sydney, Australia. Signing in with Google uses Google's own sign-in page; ClassGuard never sees your Google password.

Parents

With ClassGuard Pro, a student can add a parent from Settings → Account & devices → Parents. The parent signs in at the parent page with their own Google account and types a one-time code. Until then nothing is shared.

Once linked, each computer sends a short daily summary and some alerts to ClassGuard's account service, where only that student and their linked parents can read them:

  • Homework counts for each day: due, done on time, done late, overdue, still open.
  • Minutes in focus sessions and minutes distractions were locked.
  • Upcoming assessments: the subject name and how many days away.
  • Alerts: protection stopped while the computer was on, ClassGuard quit or removed, Holiday Mode turned on, PIN locked out after wrong tries, a parent removed.
  • Requests for a break or holiday mode, with the optional reason, and the parent's answer.

Never shared: what homework says, websites visited, apps used, or anything typed. Summaries are kept for 13 weeks, alerts for 90 days and requests for 30 days, then deleted automatically.

A parent can stop seeing a student at any time from the parent page. A student can remove a parent with their PIN; the parent is told, and sees nothing new after that. Students can see exactly what's shared under "What parents see" in ClassGuard.

Payments

ClassGuard Pro payments are handled by Stripe. You (or a parent) pay on Stripe's own page; your card details go to Stripe and never to ClassGuard. Stripe sends ClassGuard only whether the subscription is active and when it renews. Receipts, card changes and cancelling are on Stripe's subscription page, reachable from Settings → Account → Manage subscription.

What ClassGuard does not do

  • No advertising, tracking or analytics.
  • It doesn't upload your homework, timetable, browsing, or which apps you use.
  • It doesn't sell or share your information.

Updates

Installed copies check ClassGuard's GitHub releases for updates twice a day. GitHub sees that a download was requested, as with any website.

Deleting your account

Sign out and remove your devices from Settings → Account. To delete the account and everything it stores, open an issue on GitHub asking for deletion. Don't include personal details there; we'll reply with a private way to confirm.

Students under 18

ClassGuard is made for students. If you're under 18, check with a parent or guardian before creating an account. The account holds only the details above.
