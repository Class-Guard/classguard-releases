What ClassGuard does, and doesn't

  • ClassGuard helps students focus by blocking chosen websites, games and apps during class, until homework is done, and during focus sessions, on the computers it's installed on.
  • It isn't perfect. No blocking software catches everything: new websites, other browsers, other devices, or an account with administrator rights on the computer can get around it. ClassGuard is a study aid, not a guarantee, and not a replacement for a parent's or teacher's supervision.
  • ClassGuard changes some computer settings to block things (the hosts file, browser settings, pausing or closing chosen apps). These are undone when it unlocks or is removed.
  • ClassGuard updates itself. Features may change, be added or be removed over time.
