What it does

Follows your timetable

Locks distractions during class and unlocks for breaks and free periods. Works with week A/B and any school's bell times.

Homework comes first

Unfinished homework keeps games locked, all the time, in school hours, or until a time you pick. Reminders tell you what's due tomorrow.

Hard to talk your way around

Changing the rules, ending focus early or marking work done needs a PIN. Your PC and Mac stay in sync on home Wi-Fi.

Setting it up

Run the installer

Open ClassGuard-Setup.exe and choose Install. Windows asks for administrator approval once.

Choose a PIN

ClassGuard opens by itself. Pick a 6–12 digit PIN that the person studying doesn't know.

Add your timetable

Enter bell times and subjects, then choose which games and websites to block.

Move it to Applications

Open ClassGuard-Mac.zip and drag ClassGuard into your Applications folder.

Open it and set up blocking

Open ClassGuard, choose Set up blocking and approve the administrator prompt.

Choose a PIN and timetable

Settings walks you through your PIN, bell times, subjects and what to block.

For parents

Know it's working, without looking over their shoulder.

  • Daily summaries on your phone: homework on time, focus time, what's coming up.
  • Alerts if protection is turned off, ClassGuard is quit, or the PIN is guessed.
  • Break requests: approve with a time limit, or decline with a note.
  • Private by design: parents see summaries, never your own homework notes, websites or apps.

Before you install

Your computer will warn you. ClassGuard isn't code-signed yet. That's a certificate we haven't bought yet, not anything wrong with the download.

Windows: when you see "Windows protected your PC", choose More info then Run anyway.

Mac: if macOS says it can't check ClassGuard, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and choose Open Anyway. Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

Once installed, ClassGuard updates itself. Every update is signed by ClassGuard and checked before it installs. You can check any download against the SHA-256 listed on the release page.
